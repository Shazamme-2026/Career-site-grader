Analysis — what the numbers say

  1. Performance is the whole story. SEO (90 avg) and accessibility (86 avg) are broadly healthy across the cohort; performance (52 avg) is where everyone bleeds — 14 of 24 sites score under 60. The platforms differentiate on speed, not on SEO or markup.
  2. The two Shazamme sites win performance outright. Sutherland 93 and Tollgroup 91 are the only sites in the 90s, with sub-100ms blocking time and near-zero layout shift. Every competing platform's flagship is 30–70 points slower on the metric users actually feel. Only soft spot: accessibility (Sutherland 69) and Tollgroup best-practices (54) — cheap wins to lock in a clean sweep.
  3. Volcanic is the worst-performing platform (perf avg 27): Sharp & Carter 17 (LCP 16s, CLS 0.52) and Ambition 37 (LCP 19s). Both are effectively broken on mobile load — a strong competitive contrast.
  4. Worst individual offenders: Main-thread jank (TBT) — BHP 10.1s, Dunhill 7.4s, Smiggle 6.1s. Slow hero (LCP) — Camino 27s, Ambition 19s, Sharp&Carter 16s. Layout shift (CLS) — SCR Hired 0.77, Sharp&Carter 0.52, Pearl 0.40; all three fail Core Web Vitals on CLS alone.
  5. Job-board / apply link-outs correlate with the worst scores. Pearl (flagged "link-out JA jobboard / bad architecture") lands #17 with CLS 0.40; the linkout-style corporate sites (BHP, Smiggle, Sharp&Carter) cluster at the bottom. The embedded-ATS approach (Shazamme Sutherland/Tollgroup, Applyflow TRS) consistently outperforms.
  6. SEO laggards worth a closer look if they're prospects: Smiggle 67, AVID 69, BHP 75 — the only sites materially below the 90 cohort SEO average.
Method note: each site was graded once (single Lighthouse run, mobile, cold cache). Lab performance scores vary ±5–10 points run-to-run, so treat the four category numbers as directional; the LCP / TBT / CLS field diagnostics are the reliable signal. Re-running bottom-tier sites 3× and taking the median, or adding a desktop pass, is available on request.
